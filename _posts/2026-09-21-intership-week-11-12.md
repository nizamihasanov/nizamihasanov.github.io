## Week 11 - Deployment work

To deploy an internal tool, I worked with IT to set up containerized development dependencies on my workstation. This involved standard troubleshooting around virtualization prerequisites.

During setup I noticed the new tooling expanded local capabilities beyond what endpoint policy seemed to intend. I tested boundaries carefully and minimally, documented what I did, and reported it for hardening review. Lesson that stuck: developer-tooling decisions are security decisions, productivity and least-privilege have to be designed together.

## Week 12 - Monitoring support

I helped with asset monitoring dashboards and follow-ups with system owners to clean up inventory data.

Mostly coordination work, but important for keeping security visibility accurate.
