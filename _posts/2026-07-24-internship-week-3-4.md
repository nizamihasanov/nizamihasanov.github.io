## Week 3 - Access Reviews

I helped with periodic access reviews — checking whether system access still aligns with people's current roles. Because there were a lot of different internal systems and a lot of records, I helped clean up review files and automated parts of the comparison via python scripting.

Some cases still required manual review and coordination with other teams to confirm business justification.

Incredibly frustrating, but incredibly important work at the same time.

## Week 4 - Access Reviews (continuation)

Access reviews continued into week 4 due to edge cases. I also helped coordinate findings with other departments. Process dependencies caused some delays.

I always thought security auditing work is nothing complicated, but doing it at scale across many systems is harder than it looks.
