## Week 3 - Access Reviews

I was given incredibly messy excel files and role matrices and was asked to find anomalies and violations. Because there were a lot of different internal systems and a lot of records for each of them, I cleaned up excel files and automated the process via python scripting. 

There have been some instances where someone shouldn't have had certain access to a certain system based on their role at the company, but they justified their reason to gain access via tickets or email. So, on top of all the automation, I still had to manually look through some cases to ensure nothing was out of the order.

Incredibly frustrating, but incredibly important work at the same time. I even offered to further automate entire access review process and retiring excel for good, but I was told they were already working on it.

## Week 4 - Access Reviews (continuation)

There were a lot of edge cases and headaches with reviewing accesses as stated above, so this took a good chunk of my time. Not to mention, I also had to coordinate findings with other departments in the company. Bureaucracy also caused some delays.

I always thought security auditing work is nothing complicated, but if I had to deal with such issues every time a company hired me, I would burn out.
