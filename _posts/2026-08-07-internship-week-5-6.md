## Week 5 - DLP monitoring

During week 5, the workload was relatively light. I resolved some tickets here and there. One of the responsibilities of the GRC division was helping monitor data loss prevention (DLP) alerts and following up per internal process.

While doing that authorized follow-up, I stress-tested a common workflow using only dummy test data and found a case that did not alert as I expected. That gut-punch moment taught me detective controls need constant validation, not just deployment. I stopped testing immediately, documented scope and test data, and reported it the same day for remediation.

## Week 6 - IT Risks

The workload was still light during week 6, so I took tasks from another division in Information Security department, namely IT Risks. I helped review release management processes from a risk perspective and helped document potential risks for further review.
